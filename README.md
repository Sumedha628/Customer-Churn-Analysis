# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis and Prediction
## Overview
Customer churn is a major challenge in the telecommunications industry, as losing customers directly impacts revenue and increases acquisition costs. This project focuses on analyzing telecom customer data to identify churn patterns, understand customer behavior, and support data-driven retention strategies.

## Problem Statement 
Customer churn results in significant revenue loss and increased customer acquisition costs. Businesses require a systematic approach to analyze customer data, identify high-risk customer segments, understand the factors contributing to churn, and develop predictive models capable of forecasting future customer attrition.  

## Project Agenda
- Analyze customer demographics, geography, account details, and service usage.
- Study customer churn behavior and identify key churn drivers.
- Create interactive dashboards to track customer and churn metrics.
- Develop predictive models to forecast future customer churn.

## Dataset Details 
The dataset contains --
- 6,418 customer records (rows)
- **32 features** (columns) - Describing customer demographics, account information, subscribed services, billing details and churn status.

## Tools used 
- **SQL Server** - Data transformation and Analysis
- **PowerBI** - Built interactive dashboard to visualize churn trends
- **Python** - Developed Machine Learning model to predict future churn trends 

## Conclusion
- The ML model predicted 378 customers at high risk of churn and leaving the telecom service.
- Customers with Month-to-Month contracts showed the highest churn risk, making contract type the strongest predictor of churn.
- Shorter tenure (especially 0–24 months) customers and those aged 35 years and above represented the largest share of predicted churners.
- Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh recorded the highest number of predicted churn cases with highest regional risks.

## Future recommemdations 
- Implement a proactive customer retention program and offering personalized incentives before they leave.
- Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts through discounts, loyalty rewards, or bundled service plans to improve customer retention.
- Strengthen customer onboarding and engagement with providing personalized support and targeted offers for customers aged 35+ to improve long-term loyalty.
- Prioritize retention campaigns and service quality improvements in the high-risk regions, using localized marketing strategies and customer support initiatives.
